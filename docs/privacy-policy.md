## 5. Special category data (political opinions)

The text you submit, and the questions you choose to check, can reveal your
political opinions. Under Article 9 of the GDPR this is special category data
and needs an additional condition to be processed.

Our condition is your **explicit consent** (Article 9(2)(a)). When you first use
the Service you will be asked to give clear, specific consent before any of your
questions or answers are logged. If you do not consent, you should not submit
content you consider sensitive. You can withdraw consent at any time (see
section 9), which stops future logging and lets you request deletion of past
logs.

## 9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- **access** the personal data we hold about you,
- request **rectification** of inaccurate data,
- request **erasure** ("right to be forgotten"),
- request **restriction** of processing,
- **data portability** for data you provided to us,
- **object** to processing based on legitimate interests,
- **withdraw consent** at any time, without affecting processing done before
  withdrawal.

To exercise any of these, contact **[PRIVACY CONTACT EMAIL]**. We will respond
within one month.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Swedish data protection
authority, **Integritetsskyddsmyndigheten (IMY)**, at https://www.imy.se, or with
your local supervisory authority.

## 10. Withdrawing consent

You can withdraw your consent to logging from the Service settings or by
contacting us. After withdrawal we stop logging new questions and answers, and
on request we delete the logs already associated with you.

## 11. Automated processing and accuracy

Verdicts are produced automatically by AI models and are **not authoritative**.
They do not make any legally significant or similarly significant decision about
you within the meaning of Article 22. Always check the linked sources and judge
for yourself.

## 12. Security

We use technical and organisational measures appropriate to the sensitivity of
the data, including encryption in transit, access controls on the backend, and
keeping all provider API keys server-side and never in the client. No system is
perfectly secure, but we work to protect your data and to notify you and the
authorities of any breach as required by law.

## 13. Children

The Service is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect
data from children. If you believe a child has provided us data, contact us and
we will delete it.
